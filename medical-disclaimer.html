Medical Disclaimer

Last reviewed: September 20, 2026

NeoBili Pro and the resources on this website are intended as clinical support and educational reference materials for qualified healthcare professionals.

Not a Replacement for Clinical Judgment

NeoBili Pro does not replace independent medical judgment, direct patient assessment, consultation of the complete official guideline, local policy, laboratory guidance, or specialist consultation. Clinical decisions must account for the full patient context.

Verify Clinical Inputs

Users are responsible for verifying all entered information, including gestational age, postnatal age, bilirubin value, units, measurement method, risk factors, treatment history, and timestamps. Review calculated results and generated reports before use or sharing.

Guideline Sources

References to AAP 2022 and NICE CG98 describe distinct published frameworks. The American Academy of Pediatrics and NICE do not endorse NeoBili Pro. Consult the official AAP guideline and official NICE guidance for authoritative recommendations.

Clinical Resource Content

Website resource pages summarize selected concepts for general professional education. They are not complete reproductions of the source guidelines and are not patient-specific medical advice. Guidance may change; verify current source material before relying on a recommendation.
