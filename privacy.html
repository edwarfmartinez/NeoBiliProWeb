Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NeoBili Pro ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the mobile application or this website.

Mobile App Information

The NeoBili Pro mobile app is designed with privacy in mind and operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information or patient data entered in the app.

Data Storage

All data entered into the app, including patient information and clinical measurements, is stored locally on your device only. This data does not leave your device and is not accessible to us or third parties through NeoBili Pro.

No Internet Connectivity Required

NeoBili Pro functions offline and does not require internet connectivity for its clinical calculations. No patient data is sent to or received from external servers.

Website Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand website usage and improve its content. The service may process information such as pages viewed, interactions with links and website features, browser and device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, and analytics identifiers or cookies. We do not use website analytics to collect patient information or clinical data entered in the NeoBili Pro mobile app.

Google processes analytics information according to its Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The website uses Google Analytics for Firebase and may link to external services, such as the App Store or official guideline sources. Interactions with those services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page or in an app update, as applicable.

Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support@valioralabs.com.
